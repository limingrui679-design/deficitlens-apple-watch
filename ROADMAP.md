# Roadmap

The roadmap separates independently actionable engineering work from evidence that requires new
people, permissions, devices, or infrastructure.

## Maintainer-controllable

- Keep Python 3.10–3.12 compatibility and deterministic release gates.
- Add a feature-compatible independent cohort only when provenance and redistribution constraints
  are documented.
- Extend parsers through synthetic fixtures before accepting new HealthKit record types.
- Preserve a school-neutral, decision-centered case catalog and explicit transfer gates.
- Keep repository, issue, release, and citation URLs synchronized with the verified public remote.

## External prerequisites

- Maintain protected remote CI, private vulnerability reporting, and verifiable release artifacts.
- Obtain independent method review and fresh-environment reproductions.
- Conduct consented usability and accessibility studies with defined protocols.
- Evaluate additional devices, populations, free-living conditions, and directly measured resting
  expenditure.
- Measure adoption or decision impact only after real deployments exist.

No item in the second list is represented as completed in the evidence ledger.
