# Code of conduct

## Our standard

DeficitLens welcomes careful disagreement. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific,
and evidence-oriented; distinguish reproducible findings from interpretation; and protect the
privacy and safety of people represented in health data.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- explaining assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations;
- criticizing methods or claims without attacking people;
- correcting an error visibly and preserving the audit trail; and
- asking before sharing another person's data or correspondence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, disclosure of private health
information, deliberate contamination of evidence, or pressure to overstate clinical or product
claims is unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when a participant publicly represents the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Decisions should record the rule applied while avoiding further disclosure of
sensitive information.

Do not place a confidential conduct report or health record in a public issue. The project has
not yet published a public repository or confidential reporting channel; that channel must be
enabled and documented before community support is announced. Until then, retain sensitive
material locally and use only a previously established private contact with the maintainer.
